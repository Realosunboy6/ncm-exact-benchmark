\caption{Ratio of median \EVDs\ to reach and hold $\fro{X-\Xs}\le10^{-8}$ for
each of the 54 cells at $n=100$ (five paired instances per cell), on one shared
logarithmic colour scale: red cells favour AGD-SDAJ-BH, blue cells the other
method, white is parity. (a)~SBB-Dual over AGD-SDAJ-BH. The ratio is organized
primarily by rank: it exceeds 1 in every $r=5$ cell and is below 1 in every
$r=50$ cell. The near-zero multiplicity $m$ modulates the level within a rank,
most visibly at $r=20$ (from $1.03$ at $m=1$ to $0.40$ at $m=20$), whereas the
separation $\rho$ has little effect. (b)~Dykstra-APM over AGD-SDAJ-BH, two
published methods. The organization is the same, because Dykstra-APM costs
between 1.6 and 2.7 times SBB-Dual in every cell, so the whole map is shifted
towards AGD-SDAJ-BH: the ratio lies between 2.7 and 5.7 at $r=5$, between 0.94
and 2.2 at $r=20$ and between 0.58 and 1.35 at $r=50$, and it falls below 1 in
14 cells, all 12 with $r=50$ and $m\in\{5,20\}$ and two with $r=20$ and
$m=20$.}
