\caption{Where pairs of methods exchange places. Each point is the fraction of
instances at one rank on which the named method reaches
$\fro{X-\Xs}\le10^{-8}$ in fewer \EVDs\ than AGD-SDAJ-BH; a run that never
reaches the target counts as the more expensive one, and the dashed line marks
parity. (a)~SBB-Dual: the exchange happens between $r=5$ and $r=20$ at
$n=100$, between $r=50$ and $r=75$ at $n=500$, and has not been reached by
$r=100$ on the equity instances at $n=550$ or by $r=200$ at $n=2105$; the
rightmost point rests on four instances and its ordering reverses at
$10^{-10}$ (\cref{sec:us2105}). (b)~Without SBB-Dual: Dykstra-APM
overtakes AGD-SDAJ-BH between $r=20$ and $r=50$ at $n=100$ but not by $r=150$
at $n=500$. At $n=2105$, where each point rests on four instances,
Anderson-APM is ahead at $r=50$, behind at $r=100$ and $r=200$, and ahead again
at $r=400$, so its order is not monotone in rank. For SBB-Dual the rank at
which the exchange occurs rises with dimension, and for Dykstra-APM the
exchange has not occurred by $r=150$ at $n=500$; the SBB-Dual exchange is
reported as a bracket per dimension and no trend is fitted to it.}
